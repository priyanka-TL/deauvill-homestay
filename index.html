Our Story

A Home Where Kerala's Heart Beats

Founded in 2008 by NG Pradeep, Deauvill Homestay was born from a simple dream: to share the warmth, beauty, and soul of Varkala with the world. What began as a family home has grown into a beloved destination for travellers seeking authentic Kerala hospitality.

Set against Kerala's lush greenery, our homestay is a sanctuary of calm — where the rhythm of life slows, the air smells of spice and sea, and every guest is treated as family.

Varkala Life

Immersive Experiences Await

From ancient cliff-top temples to sun-soaked beaches, Varkala offers unforgettable moments — all just steps from your room.

Varkala Cliff Beach

A 2-minute walk to Kerala's most iconic laterite cliff beach — swim, surf, or watch the sun sink into the Arabian Sea.

Ayurvedic Wellness

Rejuvenate at world-class Ayurvedic centres steps from our door. Kerala's ancient healing traditions are right here.

Janardhana Swami Temple

Visit the 2,000-year-old cliffside temple — one of Kerala's most sacred and architecturally stunning pilgrimage sites.

Fresh Seafood & Local Cuisine

Cliff-top restaurants serve catch-of-the-day seafood, Kerala fish curry, and freshly brewed chai.

Local Markets & Shopping

Explore vibrant local markets, spice stalls, and artisan shops — all within easy walking distance.

Yoga & Sunrise Sessions

Join cliff-top yoga sessions as the sun rises over the sea — a ritual you'll carry home forever.
